Scroll Restoration Test Suite

Manual Test Instructions

  1. Open your app in a browser
  2. Navigate to a category/collection page (e.g., /collection or /category/topwear)
  3. Scroll down to find a product
  4. Note the scroll position (you can use browser DevTools)
  5. Click on a product to navigate to product page
  6. Press the browser back button
  7. Expected: Page should restore to the exact scroll position where you clicked
  8. Actual: Check if it scrolls to top or restores position
